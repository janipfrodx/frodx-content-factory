# Typography

Typography is one of the loudest "this was translated" tells. Get it right per target language.

## English

- Percent: **no space** before the sign - `70%`, `8.5%`, `98.6%`.
- Decimals: **point** - `8.5`. Thousands: **comma** - `10,000`.
- Quotation marks: typographic curly double quotes - “ … ”. Apostrophes: ’ (curly), not '.
- Dashes: FrodX **never uses the em dash (—, U+2014)** - it is almost always an autocorrect slip, and it reads as imported, not native. Use the spaced hyphen " - " for asides on the blog and in columns ("rhythm of a company - and sometimes…"); use the **en dash (–, U+2013)** in newsletters. If a - sneaks in, swap it for the right one for the medium.
- Currency: symbol before the number, comma thousands - €1,200, €6,000.
- Headings: pick one case convention and keep it across all headings (sentence case reads cleaner and matches the other languages; do not mix Title Case H1 with sentence-case H2).
- Spelling register: pick British/international or American and stay consistent (don't mix "recognize" with "licence"). Default to British/international for an Adriatic/DACH audience.

## Croatian

- Percent: **space** before the sign - `70 %`, `8,5 %`.
- Decimals: **comma** - `8,5`. Thousands: **period** or thin space - `10.000`.
- Quotation marks: FrodX's live HR blog is **inconsistent** - most columns use English-style “ … ” (curly high), some use the prescriptive Croatian „ … " (low-opening). They are mixed across posts. Default to “ … ” to match the EN versions and the dominant blog style; „ … " is the correct prescriptive alternative. Never mix the two within one piece; confirm FrodX house style and standardize.
- Currency: euro (Croatia is on the euro), not kuna. Format: number then word - 1.200 eura, 6.000 eura (period thousands).
- Dashes: same rule as EN - **no em dash (—)**; spaced hyphen " - " for asides on the blog ("ritam tvrtke - a taj ritam…"), en dash (–) in newsletters.
- Clitics: keep enclitics (je, su, se, mu, ga, bi…) in second position. "Vaš CRM je dosad bio bilježnica", not "Vaš CRM bio je dosad bilježnica". Wrong clitic placement is a classic non-native tell.

## Slovenian (source / reference)

- Percent: **space** - `70 %`. Decimals: **comma** - `8,5`. Quotation marks: » … «. (Relevant only if producing Slovenian; this skill targets EN and HR.)

## All languages

- Replace straight quotes ("...") and straight apostrophes with typographic ones.
- Keep product and brand names exactly as in terminology.md, regardless of surrounding typography.
- One quotation-mark style per document. Do not mix conventions within a single piece.
