# Forbidden Phrases and Patterns

## General rule

Do not use these phrases or their direct English/Croatian equivalents unless they appear inside a required quotation or the user explicitly asks to preserve them.

Replace them with natural, specific, context-aware wording.

## Replacement principles

Instead of forbidden phrases:

- Say what actually happens.
- Name the business consequence.
- Use concrete nouns and verbs.
- Prefer challenge over pain point.
- Prefer change, shift, turning point, or preokret over paradigm shift.
- Prefer what works in our experience over best practice.
- Prefer use over leverage.
- Prefer practical, focused, or specific over empty strategic adjectives.

## AI-style artifacts to remove

Remove or rewrite copy that sounds like generic AI output:

- Balanced but empty paragraphs.
- Repetitive three-part lists.
- Overuse of "not only... but also".
- Abstract benefits without operational detail.
- Inflated adjectives such as transformative, seamless, robust, cutting-edge, innovative, powerful, unless justified by the source.
- Summary endings that restate the obvious.
- Formulaic transitions such as moreover, furthermore, additionally in conversational copy.
