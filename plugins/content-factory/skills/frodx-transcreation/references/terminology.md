# Terminology

## Company and brand names

Use these names exactly:

- FrodX
- Kinetara
- SAP Engagement Cloud
- HubSpot
- Breeze Customer Agent
- Breeze Prospecting Agent
- Service Hub
- Sales Hub
- Marketing Hub
- OpenLoyalty
- GaaS
- InstantFeedback

Use "SAP Engagement Cloud" as the default term. Use "formerly SAP Emarsys" only when needed for clarity. Do not use "Emarsys" as a standalone current product name unless the source context explicitly requires reference to the former name.

## Positioning

FrodX is not just a software implementer. Present FrodX as a partner for customer-centric transformation across business, content, and technology.

Do not reduce FrodX to:

- an implementation vendor;
- a marketing agency;
- a HubSpot reseller;
- a technical integrator only;
- a generic digital transformation consultancy.

## Industries

Allowed industry references:

- finance;
- retail and e-commerce;
- energy, infrastructure, and telecommunications;
- utilities;
- automotive;
- construction;
- hospitality;
- publishing;
- technology and business solution providers.

When finance is relevant, mention DORA compliance only if supported by the source or context.

Do not invent industry expertise outside this list.

## Product and solution buckets

When relevant, preserve or map content into these solution areas:

- Kinetara: voice AI.
- AI Support / Service Hub + Breeze Customer Agent.
- AI Lead / Prospecting: Sales Hub + Marketing Hub + Breeze Prospecting Agent.
- Omnichannel / Loyalty: SAP Engagement Cloud + OpenLoyalty + GaaS.
- CX and InstantFeedback: use response-rate claims only when present in the source or explicitly provided.

- stranka -> customer or client depending on relationship

## Croatian language guardrails

Prefer:

- tjedan over nedelja
- uvjet over uslov
- sudionik over učesnik
- tvrtka over kompanija when the tone should be natural Croatian business copy
- rješenje over solucija unless the source intentionally uses a product-category term
- sastanak over meeting unless the English term is deliberately part of the style
- korisnik/klijent/kupac based on actual relationship, not as interchangeable words

Avoid unnecessary Croatian-English hybrids such as "leverageati", "skalati", "boostati", "deliverati", or similar unless the source intentionally uses informal jargon and the user wants it preserved.

## Terms to handle carefully

Do not translate product names unless an official localized name exists in the source context.

Do not overuse English business terms inside Croatian copy. Use Croatian where it sounds natural, and keep English only for established product, platform, or category names.

Do not use "pain point" or direct equivalents. Use challenge, problem, friction, barrier, blind spot, izazov, problem, prepreka, slijepa točka, or another natural alternative depending on context.

## Serbian-form traps (explicit, HR market)

Beyond the guardrails above, watch these specific Croatian↔Serbian splits - a frequent non-native tell. Croatian, not Serbian:

- suradnja, not saradnja
- tko / netko / svatko, not ko / neko / svako
- tisuća, not hiljada
- ijekavica throughout: vijesti not vesti, mlijeko not mleko, vrijeme not vreme, dijete not dete
- točno not tačno; općenito not uopšteno
- Croatian month names (siječanj, veljača, ožujak, …, prosinac), not decembar/januar, unless the source quotes them
- euro, not the old kuna (Croatia is on the euro)

Serbian vocabulary is allowed only inside a source quote or for an explicitly RS-targeted piece. Default for HR is Croatian-native.

## Dogovorjene preslikave - CX / loyalty (SL · EN · HR)

Ustaljene preslikave iz dela na loyalty/CX kolumnah. Uporabi jih dosledno:

- program zvestobe -> loyalty programme -> program vjernosti
- kazen za zvestobo -> loyalty penalty -> kazna za vjernost
- logika nagrajevanja (NE "bonitetna logika" - interno) -> reward logic -> logika nagrađivanja
- zmanjšanje tveganja -> risk reduction -> smanjenje rizika
- building society (npr. Nationwide) -> building society -> **građevinsko društvo** (NE "stambena/građevinska štedionica"; lahko ostane izvirni "building society")
- članarina -> membership fee -> članarina
- naročnina / samodejna dostava -> subscription / autoship -> pretplata / automatska dostava

Pri primerih znamk upoštevaj bralca: znamke, samoumevne v ZDA (npr. Costco), so v regiji manj poznane - po potrebi dodaj kratko pojasnilo ali izberi bolj domačo znamko (IKEA, Amazon Prime), a ohrani isti mehanizem.

## Typography

Per-language quotation marks, percent spacing, decimals, dashes, and currency are governed by `typography.md`. Apply it on every transcreation - typography is one of the loudest "this was translated" tells.
