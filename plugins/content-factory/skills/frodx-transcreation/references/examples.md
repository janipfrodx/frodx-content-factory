## Real aligned passages (EN ↔ HR, same source)

### Hook - narrative opening (missed-call)

EN: "Last week, I listened to a series of call center recordings. Listen to enough of those calls and you start to hear the rhythm of a company - and sometimes that rhythm is painful. One customer called five times in a row because she urgently needed service. She was not asking for a discount. The phone rang and rang. No one picked up."

HR: "Prošli tjedan preslušavao sam seriju snimki iz pozivnog centra. Kad dovoljno dugo slušate takve razgovore, počnete čuti ritam tvrtke - a taj ritam ponekad zna biti mučan. Jedna je stranka pet puta zaredom zvala jer joj je hitno trebao servis. Nije tražila popust. Telefon je zvonio bez odgovora. Nitko se nije javio."

Same short, uneven rhythm in both. The spaced hyphen " - " is the FrodX-house dash.

### Concrete example + numbers (CRM-machines)

EN: "Take Walmart. They already use an AI system (Pactum) for autonomous negotiations with 20,000 'long-tail' suppliers... the algorithm closed negotiations on its own, delivering 1.5% savings and a 64% deal-close rate. No human calls. No lunches. No 'how are the kids?'"

HR: "Pogledajmo konkretan primjer: Walmart. Već danas koriste AI sustav (Pactum) za autonomno pregovaranje s 20.000 dobavljača tzv. Long-Tail... Algoritam je samostalno zaključio pregovore, ostvario 1,5 % uštede i 64 % uspješnosti zatvaranja poslova. Bez ijednog ljudskog poziva. Bez ručka. Bez „kako su djeca"."

Note: "Take Walmart" → "Pogledajmo konkretan primjer: Walmart". Number formats switch - 20,000 / 1.5% / 64% (EN) ↔ 20.000 / 1,5 % / 64 % (HR). The triple punch (No… / Bez…) is preserved.

### The turning point - cost reframe (missed-call)

EN header: "The shift in perspective: AI is not a cost-cutting tool. It is a reality check."

HR header: "Preokret: AI nije trošak, nego mjerilo stvarnosti."

"preobrat" → HR "Preokret" → EN "the shift in perspective". Never a literal "turn".

### Numbers in prose (missed-call)

EN: "At an average deal value of €1,200, that is €6,000 a week slipping away simply because no one answered the phone."

HR: "Uz prosječnu vrijednost posla od 1.200 eura, govorimo o 6.000 eura tjedno koji doslovno ostaju bez odgovora."

Currency: €1,200 / €6,000 (symbol, comma) ↔ 1.200 eura / 6.000 eura (word, period).

### CTA (missed-call)

EN: "Send me three things: your approximate call volume, your business hours, and the three most common reasons customers call you. I will tell you quickly whether you are losing revenue here - or just losing patience."

HR: "Pošaljite mi tri podatka: približan volumen poziva, vaše radno vrijeme i tri najčešća razloga zbog kojih vas stranke zovu. Brzo ću vam reći gubite li ovdje novac - ili samo živce."

Specific, soft, ends on a sharp either/or. Not a generic meeting request.

### P.S. (CRM-machines)

EN: "P.S. If you think your B2B customers will always demand lunch and a handshake - remember my 2017 Webex statement."

HR: "P. S. Ako mislite da će vaši „B2B" kupci uvijek tražiti ručak i stisak ruke - sjetite se moje izjave o „Webexu" iz 2017."

### Quotation marks - note the real inconsistency

The CRM-machines HR column uses „ … " (Croatian standard): „Machine Customers", „kako su djeca". The missed-call HR column uses “ … ” (English-style). Both are live on the blog. Pick one house standard; never mix within a piece. See `typography.md`.

## Craft decisions worth copying (from the agentic-platform piece we built)

- **Translate the concept, not the words.** SL »sistem zapiskov« is NOT "system of notes" (a calque). Native EN "a system of record" (also the real industry term); native HR "sustav zapisa".
- **Signature phrases get native equivalents.** »Prevedeno v njegov jezik:« → EN "Put in his terms:", HR "Rečeno njegovim jezikom:".
- **Watch Croatian clitics and ambiguity.** "Leanpay je svoje rezultate … opisao sam" reads as "I described" - wrong; use "…opisao na vlastitom blogu". Keep enclitics in second position: "Vaš CRM je dosad bio bilježnica".
- **Drop words that exist only because of the source.** SL »po telefonu« → EN "calls" (not "calls by phone"); HR keeps the instrumental "telefonom".
- **Localize small concretes.** SL »regres« → EN "a bonus", HR keeps "regres". SL »spletna trgovina« → HR "webshop", EN "online store".

## Illustrative format patterns (no published reference)

The blog has no public examples of outbound email or web copy, so these are synthetic - use them only as format scaffolding, not as voice authority. For voice, trust the real passages above.

### Outbound email, Croatian (no meeting CTA in first email)

"Primijetio sam da financijske tvrtke sve češće ulažu u korisničku komunikaciju, ali im kanali i dalje ostaju odvojeni. Tu obično nastane problem: klijent dobije poruku, ali tvrtka ne vidi cijeli kontekst odnosa. U našoj praksi se najveći pomaci dogode tek kada se poslovni, sadržajni i tehnološki dio spoje u jedan sustav. Kako danas gledate na taj izazov kod vas?"

Starts with an observation; doesn't introduce FrodX first; ends with a relevant question, not a meeting request.

### Web copy, English (kill the generic source)

Weak source-style: "We offer a holistic approach to customer experience optimization with added value for companies that want to improve processes."

Native: "We help companies connect customer data, communication, and service workflows so teams can see the same customer reality and act faster."

Names what actually happens; removes forbidden generic language.
